## Interpret the register correctly

**OPEN** = a reproduced present blocker or deficiency. **NOT PROVEN** = an evidence gap, not evidence of absent code. **REVIEW** = a source-visible risk or discrepancy that needs first-principles confirmation before correction. **DEFERRED** = deliberately outside the current engineering lane. **EXCLUDED** = not this product. **PIN** = a known limit explicitly ordered *not* to implement in this pass. **CLOSED HERE** = fixed and checked during this inventory pass. The scale is **L** (large / integration), **M** (medium / feature), **S** (small), **T** (tiny / cosmetic), not an estimate of elapsed time. Priority P0 applies to safety and authority violations; P1 to present integration/release blockers; P2 to robustness/coverage; P3 to convenience and polish. A documented non-claim is not automatically an instruction to build it.
